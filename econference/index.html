About the conference

Economic research for an inclusive and sustainable future

A proposed gathering of economists, researchers, students and policymakers to explore the economic challenges facing Bangladesh and the wider world.

The conference concept connects research with policy dialogue through paper presentations, thematic discussions and opportunities for academic exchange. The final theme and programme will be confirmed by the organisers.

Established in 1921, the Department of Economics is one of the founding departments of the University of Dhaka. Its tradition of scholarship and policy research provides the foundation for this gathering.
