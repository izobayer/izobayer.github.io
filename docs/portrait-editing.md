## Side-profile prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit target Image 1, side-profile photograph of the
man looking left. Image 2 is the sole reference for color grading, lighting,
clothing color, background. Produce ONE individual portrait photo, portrait 2:3
composition. Preserve the exact identity, recognizable face, nose profile, ear,
hair, expression, left-facing pose, skin texture, suit, shirt and tie of Image 1.
Replace its background with the same softly defocused academic corridor
atmosphere of Image 2: soft cool blue-gray window light at left, neutral gray
corridor ceiling, softly blurred light-gray columns and receding doors at right.
Match Image 2's natural warm skin color, charcoal navy suit, clean white shirt,
muted gray-green tie and photographic contrast; restore natural color to the
desaturated target. Maintain realistic hair edges and natural light.
Crop/reframe only enough for a balanced vertical portrait, keep the entire head
visible, some breathing room ahead of the face, and upper torso. Do not turn the
face toward camera, do not change identity or age, do not beautify or smooth
skin, no extra people, no lettering or watermark, no collage.
