Media, language, and public knowledge in South Asia.

I study media and communication, emerging technologies, and the cultural politics of language. My work connects Bengali literary and cultural studies with questions of AI, information environments, institutional trust, and scholarly publishing.

Publishing infrastructure

Digital scholarship

I design and implement OJS- and OMP-based scholarly publishing systems, including editorial workflows, metadata management, digital archiving, and DOI infrastructure. My work includes platforms for Shahitto Potrika, Bangla Academy journals, BHĀBANAGARA, the International Mother Language Institute, and the Dhaka University Persian Journal.
